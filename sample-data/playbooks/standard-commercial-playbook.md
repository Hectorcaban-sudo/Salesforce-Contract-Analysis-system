# Standard Commercial Contract Playbook
## Preferred Positions & Risk Guidelines

Use this playbook as the baseline for comparison during Playbook & Risk Analysis.

### Liability Limitation
- **Preferred**: Cap mutual liability at 12 months of fees paid (or $1M, whichever is greater).
- **Acceptable**: Cap at 6–12 months of fees.
- **High Risk**: Unlimited liability, or cap below 3 months of fees.
- **Fallback language**: "Each party’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim."

### Indemnification
- **Preferred**: Mutual indemnification limited to third-party IP infringement and data breach claims, with standard exclusions (consequential damages, etc.).
- **Acceptable**: Unilateral indemnification by vendor for IP and confidentiality breaches.
- **High Risk**: Broad unlimited indemnification for any claim, or indemnification that survives termination without a cap.
- **Fallback language**: "Vendor shall indemnify Customer against third-party claims arising from Vendor’s infringement of intellectual property rights, subject to the liability cap in Section X."

### Termination for Convenience
- **Preferred**: Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days written notice after the initial term.
- **Acceptable**: 60–90 days notice.
- **High Risk**: No termination for convenience, or termination only by one party.

### Governing Law & Venue
- **Preferred**: Governing law of [Customer’s home jurisdiction] with exclusive venue in customer’s courts.
- **Acceptable**: Neutral jurisdiction or vendor’s jurisdiction with non-exclusive venue.
- **High Risk**: Vendor’s jurisdiction with exclusive venue and no carve-out for injunctive relief.

### Confidentiality
- **Preferred**: Mutual confidentiality obligations surviving 3–5 years (or perpetually for trade secrets).
- **Acceptable**: 2–3 years survival.
- **High Risk**: One-way confidentiality or survival of less than 1 year.

### Data Protection / Security
- **Preferred**: Vendor maintains SOC 2 Type II (or equivalent) and provides annual report; data processing addendum required.
- **Acceptable**: SOC 2 or ISO 27001 with reasonable notice of material changes.
- **High Risk**: No security commitments or right to audit.

### Limitation of Remedies
- **Preferred**: Consequential, incidental, and punitive damages excluded except for confidentiality, IP, or data breach claims.
- **Acceptable**: Broad exclusion with carve-outs for gross negligence or willful misconduct.
- **High Risk**: No exclusion, or exclusion that removes all remedies for material breach.

### Notes for the Agent
- Flag any clause that falls into the High Risk category.
- Suggest the Preferred or Fallback language when a deviation is detected.
- Always ground the comparison in the actual contract text provided.
